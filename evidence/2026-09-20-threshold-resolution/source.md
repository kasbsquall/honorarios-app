# R.S. 000390-2025/SUNAT

Downloaded on September 20, 2026 from https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/superin/2025/000390-2025.pdf
(SUNAT's own domain, primary source). Dated Lima, December 30, 2025.

Its article 3 sets the four amounts for tax year 2026, and it is the citation the thresholds were
missing: general monthly S/ 4,010; monthly for clause b) S/ 3,208; general annual S/ 48,125;
annual for clause b) S/ 38,500. With this, the threshold is no longer derived from the UIT (Peru's
tax reference unit), and the director case falls within what the app can calculate.
